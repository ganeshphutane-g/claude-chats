# Reel #1 — "Your AI ad may not be yours"  (pre-production)

Proceeding without an approval round, as the person asked; assumptions are listed here.

## Treatment
Logline: a polished AI ad looks protected; the copyright symbol cracks; the fact check shows why;
the human part (sketches, footage, edit) is what counts; handmade, with AI.
Visual idea: two materials tell the story. Everything "AI" is clean, glassy, product-render polish
in a graphite studio. Everything "human" is pencil on paper, drawn with wobble and graphite texture.
Palette: graphite neutrals (studio), warm amber (accent, the human/authorship thread), cool blue rim
(tech). Text: Inter Display / Inter, off-white #f5f5f7, grey #a1a1a6, amber gradient for one keyword.

## Assumptions
- Silent MP4 (skill rule). Voiceover script supplied separately, retimed to these cuts.
- 30 drawn fps (not the skill's default 15) because Apple-style motion is smooth, not on twos.
- Modelled, clean style: soft gradients and light instead of visible gouache brushwork; pencil
  texture is reserved for the human-made props.
- "14 sketches" is placeholder data the person approved; must be made true before posting.
- No real logos or seals: fictional perfume brand "LUMEN", no Copyright Office seal, no Adobe/Apple marks.
